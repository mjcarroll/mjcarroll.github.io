## Search and Text You (2017)

Won grand prize at a hackathon sponsored by the National Geospatial Agency and the City of Huntsville to think about
flood-related disasters.

The winning concept was an app to do augmented reality overlay of GPS positions transmitted via text message in an Android app,
as bandwidth would be limited, and in high flood waters recognizable landmarks may be obscured.

[Highlight video](https://www.expeditionhacks.com/rocket-city-huntsville#winners-and-highlight-video)
